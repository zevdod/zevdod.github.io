Good to know

Questions

How fast will I get my design?

Flyers, event ads and social sets: first draft within 24 hours of payment. Menus: 24–48 hours. Branding: first logo concepts within 48 hours. Rush 6-hour delivery is available for single designs.

What if I don't like the first draft?

Every order includes 2 revision rounds. Tell me exactly what to change (text, colors, layout) and I'll update it. Extra rounds are $5 each.

What files do I get?

A high-resolution PNG sized for social media and a print-ready PDF. Add an editable Canva link for $10 so you can update prices or dates yourself.

Do you design in Spanish?

Yes, bilingual English/Spanish flyers and menus are available at no extra charge. Send your text in either language.

Can you print it for me?

I deliver print-ready files you can take to any print shop or upload to an online printer. Ask and I'll recommend a size and paper.

What's your refund policy?

If I can't deliver a first draft by the promised time, you get a full refund. After a draft is delivered, refunds aren't offered, but your included revisions are there to get it right.

Do I own the design?

Yes. Once paid in full, you can use the final design however you like for your business. Logos are built from original shapes and licensed fonts so they're yours to register.
